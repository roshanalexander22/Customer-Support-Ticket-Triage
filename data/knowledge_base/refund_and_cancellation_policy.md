# Refund and Cancellation Policy (POL-REF-001)

## 1. Overview and Scope
This policy governs all refund, return, and order cancellation requests across digital subscriptions, physical hardware, and consulting services offered by NovaTech Solutions.

## 2. Standard 30-Day Return Window
Customers are entitled to request a full refund or exchange for physical merchandise and select digital purchases within thirty (30) calendar days from the date of physical delivery or digital license activation.

## 3. Autonomous Refund Authority Limits
- **Autonomous Threshold:** Automated agent workflows may approve and disburse refunds up to **$50.00 USD** per order without human intervention, provided that:
  - The customer account is in good standing (fraud risk score < 0.25).
  - The request falls within the 30-day delivery window.
  - The customer has not filed more than one refund claim in the preceding 90 days.
  - The item is accompanied by valid order confirmation or photographic proof of defect.

## 4. Mandatory Human Approval Thresholds (Consequential Actions)
Human supervisor review and explicit authorization are strictly required for:
- Any refund or credit amount **exceeding $50.00 USD**.
- Refund requests submitted beyond the 30-day standard window.
- Accounts flagged with moderate to high fraud risk (score >= 0.25).
- Repeat claimants with two or more refund transactions in the trailing 90 days.
- Claims alleging courier theft, delivery dispute with signed proof, or chargeback threats.

## 5. Non-Refundable Items & Exceptions
- Activated software license keys that have exceeded fourteen (14) days from purchase.
- Digital gift cards, store vouchers, and promotional credits.
- Custom-configured or personalized enterprise hardware.
- Shipping and handling fees, unless the shipment was delayed beyond the guaranteed carrier SLA due to merchant negligence.

## 6. Cancellation of Annual Subscriptions
- Annual SaaS plans cancelled within forty-eight (48) hours of purchase or automatic renewal are eligible for a 100% full refund.
- Annual SaaS plans cancelled after 48 hours but within thirty (30) days are eligible for a prorated refund reflecting unused months minus an administrative processing fee of $15.00 USD.
