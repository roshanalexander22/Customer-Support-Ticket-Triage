# Billing and Subscription Management Policy (POL-BIL-005)

## 1. Overview and Billing Cycle
NovaTech Solutions offers monthly and annual recurring software licenses. Subscriptions automatically renew at the conclusion of each billing cycle unless cancelled prior to the renewal date.

## 2. Accidental Renewals and 48-Hour Grace Period
- If a customer contacts support within forty-eight (48) hours of an unwanted automatic renewal and has not logged more than 5 platform actions, they are entitled to an immediate full refund.
- Autonomous refund is permitted if renewal value <= $50.00 USD. If renewal value > $50.00 USD, human manager sign-off is required.

## 3. Duplicate Billing & Charge Errors
- If a customer provides evidence of duplicate billing for the same billing period, the agent verifies CRM invoice logs.
- Confirmed duplicate charges may be reversed autonomously regardless of amount up to $100.00 USD, provided invoice IDs match the merchant gateway duplicate stamp.

## 4. Retroactive Promo Codes & Discounts
- Customers contacting within 7 days of purchase who forgot to enter a legitimate public promotional coupon code can receive a billing credit adjustment up to $25.00 USD autonomously.

## 5. Chargebacks, Disputes, and Hostile Escalations
- If a customer explicitly threatens legal action, payment processor dispute (Stripe/PayPal), or credit card chargeback, the ticket priority is elevated to **Critical** and escalated immediately to the **Senior Retention & Legal Risk Team**.
- Agents must offer an empathetic, de-escalating tone, acknowledge customer frustration, and avoid confrontational language.
