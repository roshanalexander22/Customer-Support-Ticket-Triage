# Shipping, Logistics and Return Policy (POL-LOG-003)

## 1. Overview and Carrier Partners
NovaTech Solutions operates fulfillment through certified regional and national carriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL). Standard delivery SLA is 3 to 5 business days; expedited shipping SLA is 1 to 2 business days.

## 2. In-Transit Delays & SLA Breaches
- If a delivery exceeds the estimated delivery date (EDD) by two (2) business days without tracking movement, automated systems may offer a **$10.00 to $15.00 courtesy credit** to the customer's account autonomously.
- If tracking shows no scan updates for five (5) or more consecutive business days, the package is designated as "Presumed Lost in Transit".

## 3. Presumed Lost Shipments and Replacements
- For orders with value **<= $100.00 USD**, the agent may autonomously initiate a no-charge replacement order and notify fulfillment.
- For orders with value **> $100.00 USD**, initiating a replacement order or cash indemnity requires **Human Approval** from the Logistics Dispatch Supervisor.

## 4. In-Transit Address Changes and Package Intercepts
- Package reroutes or address amendments requested after an order has reached carrier sortation hubs (Status: `In Transit` or `Out for Delivery`) cannot be executed autonomously.
- Any carrier intercept action incurs a third-party fee ($17.50) and requires human authorization to confirm delivery security and prevent fraudulent redirection.

## 5. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Labels
- Automated agents are authorized to generate and email prepaid return shipping labels (RMA) for all valid return requests within the 30-day window.
- Oversized or freight items (>50 lbs) require logistics scheduling and human specialist booking.
